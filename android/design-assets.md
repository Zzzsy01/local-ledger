# 本地 UI 插画

使用内置 ImageGen 生成，非第三方产品照片。原 PNG 为 RGBA 透明背景，打包前等比例缩小至最长边 512 px，保存为无损 WebP（保留透明通道，总计 457,850 字节）。本地资源位于 `app/src/main/res/drawable-nodpi/`，仅作装饰，无业务含义；不代替用户的物品照片。

## art_journal

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: transparent hero illustration for a polished native personal notes and life journal Android app. A sculptural editorial still life: a soft sage-green linen notebook standing partly open, a terracotta orange bookmark flowing in a loose curved ribbon, an ivory pencil leaning diagonally, one small organic olive leaf sprig. Tactile matte clay and paper 3D rendering, elegant sophisticated product illustration, warm soft studio lighting, soft ambient contact shadows, restrained muted sage, ivory, warm orange palette. Three-quarter perspective, compact balanced composition with generous clear perimeter, entire objects visible, no base plane, no colored background, genuinely transparent alpha. No readable text, letters, numbers, logo, watermark, no UI, no rounded rectangle backdrop. Premium calm, not childish, not glossy plastic.
